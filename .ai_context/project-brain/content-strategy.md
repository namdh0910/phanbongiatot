# Content & Copywriting Strategy

## 🎯 Core Themes (Problem/Crop Focus)
- **Sầu Riêng (Durian)**: Vàng lá thối rễ, nứt thân xì mủ, làm bông, rụng trái non.
- **Cà Phê & Tiêu**: Tuyến trùng rễ, rệp sáp, chết nhanh chết chậm.

## ✍️ Copywriting Style ("Happy Agri" Style)
- **Tone**: Authentic, empathetic, close to the farmer ("Bà con", "Nhà vườn").
- **Language**: Extremely simple. NO dense technical jargon unless explained via relatable analogies (e.g., "rễ cây như cái miệng").
- **Focus**: Practical, real-world benefits. What to do right now.

## 🔑 SEO & Keyword Strategy
- **Keywords**: Problem + Crop ("thuốc trị rệp sáp cà phê"), Product Type + Crop, Stage + Crop.
- **Structure**: Problem diagnosis -> Why it happens -> Step-by-step solution -> Soft product pitch.
