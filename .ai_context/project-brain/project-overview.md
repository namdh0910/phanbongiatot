# Project Overview

## 🏢 Business Identity & Model
- **Name**: Phân Bón Giá Tốt
- **Industry**: Agriculture (Fertilizers, Pest Control)
- **Positioning**: Knowledge-driven commerce. A trusted expert platform connecting farmers to authentic products via consultation.
- **Core Strategy**: Solution-Based Selling (NOT traditional e-commerce). Sell solutions and results, not just products.
- **KPIs**: Increase Zalo/Phone conversion rate, grow seller base, maximize mobile engagement.

## 👨‍🌾 User Personas
### 1. Nông Dân (Farmers - The Buyers)
- **Profile**: Heavy mobile users, low tech literacy.
- **Needs**: Simple instructions, clear prices, proven effectiveness.
- **Behavior**: Trust real people and practical advice over corporate marketing.

### 2. Đại Lý & Nhà Cung Cấp (The Sellers)
- **Profile**: Need fast operations.
- **Needs**: Simple management (like Shopee), 1-step product uploads, clear order tracking.

## 💡 Core Principles
- **Mobile-First**: 80%+ users are on mobile. 1-hand operation is mandatory.
- **Simple > Beautiful**: Clarity and ease of use trump complex features.
- **Trust > Sales**: Content builds trust; trust drives direct consultation.
